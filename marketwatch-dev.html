MSL Adjust — Roll Calculator
Roll an underwater MSL down using Laura's two rules. Pick your expiration from the dropdown (auto-filled once you enter a ticker) to price the roll on the contract you hold — or leave it on Farthest LEAPS. Live mid prices. Roll each side as its own vertical — you don't have to roll both.
When do you roll? Only at a fresh bottom — never on the way down. Laura rolls an MSL down when the 4H Mean Reversion (Mean-BT) hits ≤ −2 (deeply oversold), ideally as price tests the 200-day SMA with a buy divergence. Rolling before that just spends adjustment room while the stock keeps falling.
